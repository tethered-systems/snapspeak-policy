SnapSpeak Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · App: SnapSpeak (com.snapspeak.app) · Developer: tethered.systems

In short: SnapSpeak reads text from your photos out loud, entirely on your phone. Your photos, the text found in them and the speech made from it never leave your device. We do not have servers, accounts or analytics. If you use the free version, Google shows ads and collects some data for that, as described below. A one-time purchase removes all ads.

What SnapSpeak does on your phone

  • Photos. Photos you take or choose are processed on your device to find text. A cropped copy of each scan is saved in the app's private storage so you can open it again from History.
  • Text. Text recognition (Google ML Kit, bundled on-device model) and speech (on-device voices) run entirely on your phone. Recognised text and any edits you make are stored only on your device.
  • Audio. Speech is generated and played on your device. SnapSpeak never records audio and has no microphone permission.
  • Settings. Your preferences (voice, speed, pitch and similar) are stored on your device.

We never upload, sell or share your photos, text or audio. Cloud backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to backup services either. History keeps your most recent 100 scans; older ones are deleted automatically.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Before any ad is requested, the app asks for your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices when that option is shown.

To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and ad performance.

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings. Ads never receive your photos, scanned text or audio.

If you buy Remove ads, the app no longer starts the advertising or consent services at all.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is owned, so ads can stay off, including offline.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph text. Asked when you tap Take photo. You can refuse and still choose photos from your gallery.
  • Notifications — to show play and pause controls while reading in the background.
  • Internet — used only by the Google advertising, consent and Play purchase services. The app's own features work without it.

SnapSpeak uses the Android photo picker, so it never gets access to your whole photo library.

Sharing

If you tap Share text or Copy text, the text goes to the app or clipboard you choose. That only happens when you ask for it, and images are never shared.

Deleting your data

Delete a scan by swiping it away in History. Uninstalling SnapSpeak removes all of its data from your phone. Because we keep nothing on servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data Google processes for advertising, use your Android settings or Google's controls described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

SnapSpeak is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
